# Figure captions (drafts)

- **fig1_retention**: Chance-adjusted retention of detection and attribution under additional JPEG compression for reference-trained models. Top: balanced accuracy at the frozen validation operating point (registered metric). Bottom: AUROC-based retention, threshold-free (attribution AUROC is the mean one-vs-rest AUROC over fake videos). FF++ ResNet-18 rows are the registered results; FF++ EfficientNet-B4 (X6) and FF++ AUROC curves (X1a) are post-freeze extensions. DFDM curves are the separately registered study. Lines are seed means; whiskers span the three seeds. Retention above 1 (DFDM detection at Q95–Q80) means BA rose above its reference value: the reference-only DFDM models have frozen thresholds near 0, so their reference BA is depressed while detection AUROC stays near 100%.
- **fig2_granularity**: Retention by attribution granularity at JPEG Q60 and Q20 for the reference-trained ResNet-18. FF++: detection, identity-swap vs reenactment family, Deepfakes vs FaceSwap, four-way methods, Face2Face vs NeuralTextures (content-matched decomposition C3c). DFDM: detection and five-way generator attribution. Deepfakes vs FaceSwap is retained best; Face2Face vs NeuralTextures loses most within FF++, and the five closely related DFDM generators fall to chance by Q60. Retention is chance-adjusted, so tasks with different chance levels are comparable.
- **fig3_confusion**: Attribution confusion matrices of the reference-trained ResNet-18 at the reference condition and at JPEG Q20 (rows: true class; three seeds pooled). FF++: DF Deepfakes, F2F Face2Face, FS FaceSwap, NT NeuralTextures. DFDM: FSW Faceswap, LW Lightweight, IAE, DFK DFaker, H128 DFL-H128. At Q20, FF++ reenactment methods drift towards NT. In DFDM, each seed collapses onto one or two generators regardless of the true class (seed 42 mostly DFL-H128, seed 43 Lightweight and DFL-H128, seed 44 DFaker), so the pooled Q20 rows are nearly identical.
- **fig4_delta_auc**: Threshold-free contrast Δ_AUC(Q20) = attribution minus detection AUROC retention, with 95% group-bootstrap intervals. FF++ values are post-freeze extensions (X1a, X7, X8); DFDM values are the registered secondary contrast D-S1. DFDM frozen probes are omitted because their reference attribution is near chance.
- **fig5_e10**: External detection test E10: frozen FF++ detectors on the 518 official Celeb-DF v2 test videos, AUROC under additional JPEG compression (seed means).
